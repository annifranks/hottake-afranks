Ice cream is better in a cup

When eating ice cream it is much better to eat it out of a cup rather than cone.

Learn More

More Toppings

Ice cream is better in a cup because you can add more toppings without worrying about them falling off. A cup gives you more space to pile on toppings like sprinkles, chocolate chips, candy, or whipped cream.

Learn More

Easier to Eat

Ice cream is easier to eat from a cup because you can use a spoon and take your time. You do not have to worry about biting into a cone or trying to keep the ice cream from falling off the sides.

Learn More

Less Messy

Ice cream is less messy when served in a cup because it does not melt through the bottom or drip down your hands. You can enjoy your ice cream without worrying about making a sticky mess.
